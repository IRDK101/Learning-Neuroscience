## One family, one assembly line {#family}

Dopamine (DA), norepinephrine (NE) and epinephrine (adrenaline) are called **catecholamines** because they all share a chemical "catechol" group. They come from the same synthesis chain:

::pathway{id="catecholamine-synthesis"}

- **Tyrosine** is the precursor of all three.
- **Tyrosine hydroxylase** turns tyrosine into **L-DOPA**. This is the **rate-limiting step** for the whole family.
- **DOPA decarboxylase** turns L-DOPA into **dopamine** {{C-022}}.
- **Dopamine β-hydroxylase (DBH)**, which sits *inside the vesicles*, turns dopamine into **norepinephrine**.
- **PNMT**, which works in the *cytosol*, turns NE into **epinephrine**.

Each neuron releases only **one** of these. Which one depends on the enzymes it has: no DBH means it stops at dopamine, DBH means NE, and DBH plus PNMT means epinephrine.

::check{q="q-nt-th-rate"}

## Dopamine {#dopamine}

::diagram{id="nt-overlay" layers="da,deep" highlight="substantia-nigra,ventral-tegmental-area"}

Dopamine neurons live in the **midbrain** (upper brainstem), in two groups:

::focus{highlight="substantia-nigra,striatum" layers="da,deep"}

- The **substantia nigra** sends dopamine to the **striatum** (basal ganglia), where it's needed to start movements.

::focus{highlight="ventral-tegmental-area,prefrontal-cortex" layers="da,deep"}

- The **ventral tegmental area (VTA)** sends dopamine to the **frontal lobe**.

Dopamine is widespread but doesn't cover the whole cortex; most of it stays in the striatum. Its job is less "feeling happy" and more **learning what makes us happy**, creating incentives to go after it. That's why it plays a big part in **addiction**.

::focus{highlight="substantia-nigra" layers="da,deep"}

**Parkinson's disease** happens when substantia nigra neurons die and the striatum loses its dopamine. The result is trouble starting movements and a tremor at rest. Treatment is **L-DOPA**: unlike dopamine, it can cross the **blood–brain barrier**, and surviving neurons turn it into dopamine. It can't replace lost cells, so it only works while enough cells remain.

::check{q="q-nt-parkinsons"}

## Clearing dopamine, and the drugs that interfere {#dopamine-drugs}

::diagram{id="synapse" highlight="reuptake-transporter,monoamine-oxidase"}

After release, dopamine is pulled back into the terminal by the **dopamine transporter (DAT)**. Inside, some is repackaged into vesicles and some is broken down by **MAO**, an enzyme on the mitochondria.

::focus{highlight="reuptake-transporter"}

- **Cocaine** blocks the dopamine transporter, so dopamine lingers in the synapse and keeps activating receptors. Over time the neurons desensitize and the effect wears off.
- **Amphetamines** (including meth) block the transporter *and* get into the terminal through it. Once inside, they break dopamine out of its vesicles so it leaks out **without an action potential**.

::focus{highlight="monoamine-oxidase,mitochondrion"}

- **MAO inhibitors** block MAO so less dopamine (and NE and serotonin) is broken down. They're used for depression and anxiety.

::check{q="q-nt-amphetamine-vs-cocaine"}

## Norepinephrine {#norepinephrine}

::diagram{id="nt-overlay" layers="ne,deep" highlight="locus-coeruleus"}

To make NE, a neuron first makes dopamine; then **DBH inside the vesicle** converts it. Like dopamine, NE is cleared by reuptake through a transporter and then repackaged or broken down by MAO.

NE comes mainly from the **locus coeruleus** in the pons. Its name means "blue spot", because it looks bluish. It's a tiny, concentrated group of cells with enormous reach: thalamus, hypothalamus, cortex, hippocampus, cerebellum, spinal cord.

NE helps regulate **sleep–wake cycles** and **autonomic functions**, and is involved in **learning, memory and mood**.

::check{q="q-nt-dbh"}

## Epinephrine (adrenaline) {#epinephrine}

Epinephrine is one step past NE. Because **PNMT works in the cytosol**, NE has to leave the vesicle, get converted, and then be repackaged as epinephrine. It's cleared like NE (reuptake, or MAO), and its effects are similar: sleep–wake, and extra arousal when needed.

::check{q="q-nt-pnmt"}
