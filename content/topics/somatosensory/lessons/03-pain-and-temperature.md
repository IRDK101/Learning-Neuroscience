## Fast and slow fibers {#fibers}

Not all body-sense signals travel at the same speed. Wider and more myelinated axons conduct faster ([[fiber-types]]):

| Fiber | Carries | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| Aα | Proprioception (muscle spindles) | Fastest |
| Aβ | Touch (mechanoreceptors) | Fast |
| Aδ | Sharp pain, cold | Slower, thin myelin |
| C | Dull pain, heat | Slowest, no myelin |

## Pain {#pain}

[[Nociception|nociception]] is the detection of harmful stimuli by [[free nerve endings|free-nerve-ending]] near the skin's surface. Many nociceptors are **polymodal**: they respond to strong mechanical force, extreme temperatures and chemicals released by damaged cells.

Two fiber types carry pain. Stub your toe and you feel a sharp, well-localized jab first (Aδ), then a slower, dull, throbbing ache (C).

## Temperature {#temperature}

[[Thermoreceptors|thermoreceptor]] are free nerve endings tuned to different temperature ranges. The brain judges temperature by combining their signals.

Temperature and pain overlap but use different receptors. Heat-sensing thermoreceptors fire more as things warm up, then **max out a little below about 45 °C**. Around 45 °C, heat nociceptors start firing. Above that, a stove feels **more painful** without feeling any hotter.
